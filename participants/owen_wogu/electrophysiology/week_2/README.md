What is your preferred recording technique? And why?

My preferred recording techniques are multi-unit recording methods 
such as multi-electrode arrays, tetrodes, silicon probes, and 
Neuropixels. I find them interesting because they provide much more 
detailed recordings of brain activity than conventional EEG while 
helping researchers study how groups of neurons work together. As 
someone interested in neuroengineering, I hope that what we learn from 
these techniques can eventually help improve non-invasive technologies 
like EEG, making them more accurate and useful for both research and 
clinical applications. That possibility is what makes these recording 
methods the most exciting to me.
